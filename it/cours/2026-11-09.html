A new outlet near Rome

The text

Vicino a Roma c'è un nuovo posto (place · endroit) per fare acquisti (shopping · achats). Si chiama Roma Outlet Village. Ha aperto (opened · ouvert) il 6 novembre, vicino al paese (village · village) di Sant'Oreste.

Ci saranno 90 negozi (shops · magasins). Alcuni sono già aperti. Gli altri arriveranno (will arrive · arriveront) nei prossimi sei mesi (months · mois). Ci sono vestiti (clothes · vêtements), scarpe (shoes · chaussures) e posti per mangiare (to eat · manger).

Il progetto (project · projet) costa (costs · coûte) circa 100 milioni di euro. Oggi qui lavorano (work · travaillent) circa 300 persone (people · personnes). Presto saranno 400.

Nel primo fine settimana c'è stata una grande festa (party · fête). C'erano sconti (discounts · réductions) fino al 50%. C'erano anche musica e giocolieri (jugglers · jongleurs).

Ora arriva il Natale (Christmas · Noël). C'è un albero (tree · arbre (sapin)) alto 14 metri. I bambini (children · enfants) possono incontrare (meet · rencontrer) Babbo Natale. Possono anche lasciare (leave · laisser) una lettera (letter · lettre) per lui. Le feste durano (last · durent) fino al 6 gennaio.

Near Rome there is a new place to go shopping. It is called Roma Outlet Village. It opened on 6 November, near the village of Sant'Oreste.

There will be 90 shops. Some are already open. The others will arrive in the next six months. There are clothes, shoes and places to eat.

The project costs about 100 million euros. Today about 300 people work here. Soon there will be 400.

On the first weekend there was a big party. There were discounts of up to 50%. There was also music and jugglers.

Now Christmas is coming. There is a tree 14 metres tall. Children can meet Santa Claus. They can also leave a letter for him. The festivities last until 6 January.
