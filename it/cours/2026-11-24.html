Latin or Greek?

The text

Nell'Impero (Empire · Empire) romano c'erano due lingue (languages · langues) ufficiali (official · officielles). Una era il latino. L'altra era il greco. Ma quale parlava (spoke · parlait) di più la gente (people · gens)? La risposta (answer · réponse) è sorprendente (surprising · surprenante).

I popoli (peoples · peuples) conquistati (conquered · conquis) imparavano (learned · apprenaient) il latino. Lo usavano (used · utilisaient) per parlare (to talk · parler) con i capi (leaders · chefs) romani, i soldati (soldiers · soldats) e i coloni (settlers · colons). Era la lingua del potere (power · pouvoir).

In Oriente (the East · Orient) la gente parlava greco. Il greco era la lingua della filosofia (philosophy · philosophie) e dei libri (books · livres). Ma era anche (also · aussi) la lingua di tutti i giorni (days · jours). Le persone colte (educated · cultivées) preferivano (preferred · préféraient) il greco. Per loro il latino era quasi una lingua da contadini (farmers · paysans).

Nel 395 l'Impero si divise (split · se divisa) in due parti (parts · parties). Il confine (border · frontière) seguiva (followed · suivait) le lingue. A ovest (west · ouest) c'era il latino. A est (east · est) c'era il greco.

L'imperatore (emperor · empereur) Giustiniano visse (lived · vécut) dal 482 al 565. Lui scrisse (wrote · écrivit) molte (many · beaucoup de) leggi (laws · lois) anche in greco. Così tutti potevano capire (understand · comprendre) le leggi.

In the Roman Empire there were two official languages. One was Latin. The other was Greek. But which one did people speak more? The answer is surprising.

Conquered peoples learned Latin. They used it to talk with Roman leaders, soldiers and settlers. It was the language of power.

In the East, people spoke Greek. Greek was the language of philosophy and books. But it was also the everyday language. Educated people preferred Greek. For them, Latin was almost a language for farmers.

In 395 the Empire split into two parts. The border followed the languages. In the west there was Latin. In the east there was Greek.

Emperor Justinian lived from 482 to 565. He wrote many laws in Greek too. That way everyone could understand the laws.
